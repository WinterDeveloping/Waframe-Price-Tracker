Charts cover the last 90 days; hover one to see the price on a given day. The dashed line is the 30-day average. Blue means cheaper than usual, orange means pricier. Prices are the lowest sell listing, checked every hour; days before 14 Oct 2026 are warframe.market's daily median sale price.

Vaulted items' relics no longer drop, so they can only be traded for (or bought during Prime Resurgence). Farmable items' relics currently drop. Vault status comes from the community WFCD warframe-items project and refreshes weekly.

Owned and tracked items are saved in this browser only. Use Export under My items to back them up or move them to another device. Tracking targets are checked against each hourly update while the page is open; the site can't notify you when it's closed.
